School Profile

Lae Secondary School is located in Lae District, Morobe Province, Papua New Guinea. This About Us page introduces the school’s purpose, educational priorities and relationship with its community. It is designed as a central guide for students, parents, staff, education stakeholders and visitors seeking information about the school.

The school’s work centres on teaching and learning, student development, responsible conduct and preparation for future education and life opportunities. Specific operational details—including enrolment requirements, grade levels, approved programs and current services—should be confirmed with the school administration.

Our School Motto
Learning • Leadership • Service

Our History and Establishment

Lae Secondary School is a government secondary school located in Lae, Morobe Province, Papua New Guinea. Historical records indicate that secondary education was already established in Lae by 1963, reflecting the early development of formal secondary education in the city. The school has since served generations of students, supporting academic learning and preparing young Papua New Guineans for further education, employment and community leadership.

Vision, Mission and Values

A shared vision and mission help guide the school community’s priorities. The following statements are proposed draft wording for review and formal approval by the school’s authorised leadership.

Our Vision

To nurture knowledgeable, responsible and confident learners who are prepared to contribute positively to their communities and pursue future opportunities.

Our Mission

To support quality teaching and learning in a safe, respectful and inclusive environment that develops students’ knowledge, practical skills, character and sense of responsibility.

Our Values

Respect, integrity, responsibility, diligence, fairness, cooperation, care for others and a commitment to continuous learning.

These proposed statements should be checked against the school’s officially adopted strategic statements before being treated as formal policy. Read more on the Vision & Mission page.

Teaching, Learning and Academic Development

Teaching and learning are central to a secondary school’s purpose. Students need opportunities to build subject knowledge, ask questions, think critically, communicate clearly, solve problems and develop the study habits that support continued learning.

Information about the school’s current curriculum, subject departments, assessment arrangements, academic calendar and learning support should be maintained using approved and up-to-date school records. Visit Academics and Subject Departments for related information.

Student Development and Wellbeing

Education involves more than academic progress. A positive school environment also encourages self-discipline, confidence, teamwork, leadership, respect for diversity and consideration for others. Students should be supported to understand their responsibilities, seek assistance when needed and participate constructively in school life.

Current information about student leadership, sports, clubs, counselling or welfare support, boarding arrangements and student services should be confirmed by the relevant school officers. Explore the Students section for related pages.

Robotics, STEM and Innovation

Lae Secondary School has received public attention for its student robotics activities and participation in international robotics events. News reports describe the school’s teams representing Papua New Guinea at the FIRST Global Challenge and report the opening of a robotics laboratory in 2025. These experiences demonstrate how practical, team-based learning can connect science, technology, engineering and mathematics with real-world problem-solving.

Robotics activities can help students develop design thinking, programming, electronics, mechanical problem-solving, collaboration, communication and resilience. Details about current teams, projects, competition participation, laboratory access and mentoring arrangements should be confirmed by the school before publication. Visit the dedicated Robotics page for the expanded overview and linked public reports.

Achievements and Recognition

School achievement includes academic progress, sports, cultural activities, student leadership, service to the community and innovation. Public reporting has highlighted Lae Secondary School’s international robotics participation, including reported FIRST Global Challenge rankings in 2023 and 2025. These milestones sit alongside the everyday achievements of students and staff whose work strengthens the school community.

Specific academic results, individual awards, team placements and other honours should be published only after they have been checked against reliable records or authorised school announcements. The Achievements page distinguishes reported milestones from categories awaiting confirmation.

Leadership and Governance

Effective school leadership provides direction, supports staff, promotes student welfare and helps ensure that school processes are communicated clearly. Governance responsibilities and reporting arrangements should follow applicable education requirements and the school’s approved procedures.

The school website identifies Mr Christopher Raymond as Principal in the supplied site content. Details of the wider leadership team, board or governing arrangements, staff responsibilities and official reporting channels should be confirmed before publication. Visit the Leadership page for updates.

Parents, Guardians and Community Partnership

Constructive partnerships between the school, parents, guardians and the wider community can strengthen students’ learning and wellbeing. Clear communication helps families understand school expectations, follow notices, raise concerns through the appropriate channels and support learning at home.

Official contact processes, parent meetings, community engagement activities and partnership opportunities should be published when confirmed. Parents and guardians can also review the Parents information page and use the Contact page for enquiries.

Safe, accessible and well-managed facilities help support effective teaching, learning and school activities. School Standards, Rules and Policies

Consistent and clearly communicated rules help establish an orderly, respectful and supportive environment. Approved policies may address attendance, punctuality, uniform, behaviour, assessment, student protection, health and safety, use of school property and communication with families.

The current approved versions, implementation dates and any required forms should be confirmed by school administration. Refer to Policies & Rules and Downloads for published documents.
