Academic Programs Overview

Lae Secondary School offers a structured secondary curriculum aligned with the requirements of the National Department of Education. Our programs aim to develop knowledge, skills, values and attitudes that prepare students for higher education, vocational pathways and responsible citizenship.

Lower Secondary

Grade 9 & Grade 10

The lower secondary program builds on primary education and provides a broad foundation across key learning areas.

  • Core subjects including English, Mathematics, Science and Social Science
  • Practical and personal development subjects
  • Preparation for Grade 10 Certificate examinations
  • Focus on literacy, numeracy and study skills
  • Character formation and values education

Upper Secondary

Grade 11 & Grade 12

The upper secondary program deepens learning and prepares students for tertiary study, training or employment.

  • Specialised subject choices according to pathways
  • Preparation for Grade 12 Certificate examinations
  • Development of independent study and research skills
  • Career guidance and further education awareness
  • Leadership and service opportunities

Note on School Structure

For the current approved grade offerings, subject availability and examination arrangements at Lae Secondary School, please contact the school administration. Information should be confirmed by the school before publication.

Assessment & Examinations

How Student Progress Is Measured

Assessment is continuous and includes classwork, assignments, tests, practical work and formal examinations. The school follows assessment policies approved by the National Department of Education and relevant examining bodies.

  • Formative assessment – ongoing feedback to support learning
  • Summative assessment – term tests, internal examinations and final grading
  • National examinations – Grade 10 and Grade 12 Certificate examinations as applicable
  • Reporting – progress reports issued to parents/guardians at designated times

Official examination information and results guidance (where publicly available) can be found on the Results page. Confidential student results are only released through authorised channels.
