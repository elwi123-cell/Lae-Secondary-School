Achievements

Celebrating the success, effort and character of our students and school community.

Home  ›  Achievements

Achievement at Lae Secondary School is measured through learning, innovation, service, character and opportunity. The school community has demonstrated an ability to take students beyond the classroom, including through international robotics competitions, investment in STEM learning facilities and the graduation of students preparing for further education.

This page brings together publicly reported milestones and the wider areas in which student and school achievements can be recognised. Published facts are linked to their sources. Other categories are included as areas for future authorised updates—not as claims that a particular award has already been won.

Documented School Highlights

Selected milestones reported by established news organisations and the FIRST Global programme.

🤖

International Robotics Achievement

Lae Secondary School students have represented Papua New Guinea at the FIRST Global Challenge, collaborating with young people from around the world through science, technology, engineering and mathematics (STEM).

  • 2018: international competition in Mexico
  • 2019: international competition in Dubai
  • 2022: team represented PNG in Geneva, Switzerland
  • 2023: reported 43rd place among 191 participating countries
  • 2024: reported 78th place among 180 countries and territories
  • 2025: reported 90th place among 181 participating countries

Sources: The National — 2023 result; PNG Kundu — 2024 report (PDF); The National — 2025 result.

🔬

Robotics Laboratory Opened in 2025

In July 2025, Lae Secondary School opened a dedicated robotics laboratory supported by the National Department of Education. The facility provides a more stable place for students and mentors to develop projects and strengthen practical STEM learning.

  • Official opening reported on 11 July 2025
  • Education Secretary Dr Uke Kombra attended the opening
  • Reported project cost was estimated at close to K1 million, including transport and construction costs
  • The school’s robotics initiative was recognised as a way to encourage innovation and critical thinking

Sources: NBC PNG; The National.

🎓

2025 Graduation Milestone

Lae Secondary School held its 22nd graduation ceremony in November 2025, celebrating 435 students completing their school programme. The ceremony recognised students’ efforts and marked an important transition toward further study, training and employment.

  • 435 students received certificates at the ceremony
  • A female Science Dux award was reported by NBC PNG
  • School leaders encouraged graduates to remain resilient while pursuing tertiary opportunities
  • At the time of the report, official Grade 12 examination results had not yet been released; no unannounced results are claimed here

Source: NBC PNG — 19 November 2025.

🏗️

Investment in Practical Skills

School infrastructure support has also been reported. In February 2026, The National reported that a four-in-one practical skills building was under construction with funding from the Lae City Authority.

  • Lae MP John Rosso pledged K1 million during the school’s 2025 graduation ceremony
  • The February 2026 report said half of the pledged funding had been presented to help complete the building
  • The facility represents investment in practical learning infrastructure

Source: The National — school fees and infrastructure report, February 2026. Project completion should be confirmed by the school before this page is updated.
