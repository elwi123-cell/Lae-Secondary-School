Admissions

Join our school community. Learn about enrolment requirements, application procedures and important dates.

Home  ›  Admissions

Lae Secondary School welcomes applications from students who are committed to academic excellence, discipline and service. We offer quality secondary education for Grades 9–12 and provide a supportive learning environment for young Papua New Guineans.

This page outlines the admission process, required documents and key information for parents and guardians. Please read carefully and contact the school office if you need further assistance.

Who Can Apply?

We accept applications for the following entry points and transfers.

9️⃣

Grade 9 Entry

Primary school graduates seeking to begin secondary education at our school.

  • Successful completion of Grade 8
  • Meeting school selection criteria
  • Submission of required documents
  • Availability of places
1️⃣1️⃣

Grade 11 Entry

Students progressing from Grade 10 who wish to continue into upper secondary.

  • Successful completion of Grade 10
  • Satisfactory academic results
  • Subject pathway suitability
  • Availability of places in chosen stream
🔄

Transfer Students

Students transferring from other recognised secondary schools during the year.

  • Valid transfer letter / clearance
  • Academic reports from previous school
  • Meeting year-level requirements
  • Subject to available places

Required Documents

Please prepare the following documents when applying.

Documents to Submit with Application

📄 For All Applicants

  • Completed Application Form
  • Copy of Birth Certificate or official age document
  • Recent passport-size photographs (usually 2)
  • Copy of student’s Grade 8 or Grade 10 certificate / report
  • School Transfer / Clearance letter (for transfers)

👨‍👩‍👧 Additional Information

  • Parent / Guardian contact details
  • Medical information (if relevant)
  • Proof of residence (if required by the school)
  • Any special educational needs information
  • Application fee receipt (if applicable)

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about admissions and enrolment.

When can I collect or download the application form? +
Application forms are usually made available several months before the start of the new academic year. Check the News page or contact the school office for the exact opening date.
Is there an application or enrolment fee? +
Some schools charge a non-refundable application or processing fee. Please refer to the Fees & Charges page or ask the school office for the current approved charges.
Can my child transfer mid-year? +
Mid-year transfers are considered on a case-by-case basis, subject to available places, academic standing and a valid transfer/clearance letter from the previous school.
Does the school provide boarding? +
Please check the Boarding page (or contact the school) for information on boarding facilities, availability and any additional requirements or fees.
Who should I contact for admission enquiries? +
Please contact the school office during office hours (Monday–Friday, 8:00 am – 4:00 pm) by phone or email. You may also visit the Contact page for full details.
