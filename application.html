Application Form

Complete the student enrolment application form for Lae Secondary School.

Home  ›  Admissions  ›  Application Form

Please complete this application form carefully. All fields marked with * are required. You may fill the form online and print it, or print a blank copy and complete it by hand.

Submit the completed form together with all required supporting documents to the school office before the closing date. Incomplete applications may not be considered.

How to Use This Form

  1. Fill in all required sections accurately using capital letters where possible.
  2. Attach copies of supporting documents (see the Admissions page for the full list).
  3. Click Print Form below to print a completed or blank copy.
  4. Lodge the signed form at the school office during office hours.
  5. Keep a photocopy of the completed form for your own records.
