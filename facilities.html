School Facilities

General information about campus facility types. Contact the school to confirm current facilities and access.

Home  ›  School Facilities

Lae Secondary School is located in Lae District, Morobe Province, Papua New Guinea. This page outlines common types of facilities that may support secondary-school teaching, learning, student welfare and co-curricular activities; it does not confirm that each facility listed is available on the school campus.

Facility names and examples below are provided as a general guide, not as a verified inventory of Lae Secondary School buildings or equipment. Please contact the school to confirm what is currently available, its condition, access arrangements and capacity.

📚

Learning Facilities

🏫

Classrooms

General teaching classrooms for Grades 9–12, designed to support interactive and subject-based learning.

  • Well-ventilated teaching spaces
  • Blackboards / whiteboards
  • Basic teaching resources
  • Seating for standard class sizes
🔬

Science Laboratories

Practical science facilities supporting Biology, Chemistry, Physics and General Science lessons.

  • Laboratory benches and work areas
  • Basic science equipment and apparatus
  • Storage for chemicals and materials
  • Safety provisions where available
📖

Library / Resource Centre

A quiet space for reading, research and independent study, supporting literacy and academic work.

  • Fiction and non-fiction collections
  • Reference materials and textbooks
  • Study tables and reading areas
  • Access during designated library hours
💻

ICT / Computer Room

Computer facilities supporting ICT lessons, research and digital literacy skills for students.

  • Desktop computers (where available)
  • Basic software for learning
  • Teacher demonstration area
  • Scheduled class access
⚽

Sports & Outdoor Facilities

🏟️

Sports Grounds

Outdoor sports fields and playing areas used for physical education, inter-house and inter-school competitions.

  • Football / rugby field areas
  • Open spaces for athletics
  • Multi-use outdoor courts (where available)
  • Assembly and parade ground
🏐

Courts & Activity Areas

Areas designated for basketball, volleyball, netball and other court-based sports and activities.

  • Basketball / volleyball courts
  • Netball court (where available)
  • Space for recreational games
🏠

Residential & Support Facilities

🛏️

Boarding Dormitories

Residential accommodation for boarding students (where boarding is offered), with separate arrangements for boys and girls.

  • Dormitory sleeping areas
  • Basic washing and toilet facilities
  • Supervised residential environment
  • See Boarding page for more details
🏢

Administration Block

Offices for the Principal, Deputy Principals, administration staff and student services.

  • Principal’s office
  • Administration and reception
  • Staff work areas
  • Visitor reception point
🛠️

Workshops / Practical Areas

Practical facilities supporting Technology, Agriculture, Design and other applied subjects (where offered).

  • Design & Technology workshop space
  • Agriculture / garden areas
  • Storage for tools and materials
🚻

Student Amenities

Essential amenities supporting daily student life and hygiene on campus.

  • Student toilets and wash areas
  • Drinking water points
  • Canteen / tuckshop (where available)
  • Shade and outdoor seating areas

Maintaining & Improving Our Facilities

The school is committed to the ongoing maintenance, safety and gradual improvement of its facilities. Support from parents, the school board, government and development partners helps ensure that students learn in a safe and functional environment.

ℹ️

Important: This page is a general facilities guide, not an official facilities inventory. The presence, condition, equipment, capacity and accessibility of classrooms, laboratories, ICT rooms, sports areas, boarding accommodation and other facilities have not been independently confirmed. Please contact Lae Secondary School before relying on any specific facility being available or arranging a visit.
