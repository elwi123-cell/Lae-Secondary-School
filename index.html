STEM • ENGINEERING • INNOVATION

Explore the Future of Robotics in Lae Secondary School

Lae Secondary School recognizes the growing importance of Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics (STEM) in preparing students for the future. Robotics represents an exciting opportunity to introduce students to modern technology, automation, engineering principles, computer programming and innovative problem-solving.

Through robotics education and practical technology demonstrations, students can explore how robotic systems operate, how mechanical components work together, and how automation is transforming industries around the world. These learning experiences can encourage creativity, critical thinking, teamwork and a deeper interest in engineering, information technology and scientific research.

The robotic arm animation featured on this website represents the possibilities that modern technology offers to education. It reflects the importance of equipping students with the knowledge, skills and confidence to participate in Papua New Guinea’s digital transformation and future technological development.

Lae Secondary School remains committed to promoting a culture of learning, innovation and technological awareness, inspiring students to become future scientists, engineers, technology professionals and leaders who will contribute meaningfully to the development of Papua New Guinea.
