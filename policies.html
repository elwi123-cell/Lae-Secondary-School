School Policies & Rules

Guidance supporting a safe, respectful, disciplined and productive learning environment for students, staff, parents and visitors.

Home  ›  About Us  ›  Policies & Rules

Our approach to school expectations

Lae Secondary School aims to provide an environment where teaching and learning can take place with respect, responsibility, fairness and care for others. School policies help explain expected conduct, support student wellbeing and guide consistent decisions by the school community.

This page provides general guidance only. It does not replace the school’s formally approved policies, current Department of Education requirements or directions issued by the school administration. Students and families should contact the school to confirm the current official rules and procedures.

🎓

Student conduct and respect

Students are expected to contribute to a respectful and orderly school environment.

  • Treat students, teachers, staff and visitors respectfully.
  • Follow reasonable instructions from authorised school staff.
  • Respect school property and the property of others.
  • Avoid bullying, harassment, intimidation, discrimination and violence.
📚

Attendance and punctuality

Regular attendance and arriving on time support learning and classroom participation.

  • Attend scheduled classes and approved school activities.
  • Arrive on time and be prepared for lessons.
  • Have a parent or guardian communicate absences where required.
  • Confirm the school’s current process for explaining absences and requesting leave.
📝

Academic honesty

Learning and assessment should reflect each student’s own effort and understanding.

  • Complete assignments and assessments honestly.
  • Do not copy another person’s work or assist academic dishonesty.
  • Follow teachers’ instructions for examinations and assessment tasks.
  • Ask teachers for clarification when assessment rules are unclear.
🛡️

Safety, wellbeing and safeguarding

Everyone has a role in helping maintain a safe school environment.

  • Report safety concerns, threats, bullying or suspected harm to a trusted staff member.
  • Follow school instructions during drills, emergencies and evacuations.
  • Do not bring dangerous or prohibited items onto school grounds.
  • Respect the privacy, dignity and wellbeing of others.
🏫

Uniform, facilities and property

School dress expectations and care of facilities help maintain a suitable learning setting.

  • Follow the school’s current uniform and appearance requirements.
  • Keep classrooms, grounds and shared facilities clean.
  • Use equipment, laboratories, library resources and technology responsibly.
  • Report damage, hazards or lost property to the appropriate staff.
📱

Technology and communication

Digital devices and online communication should be used responsibly and lawfully.

  • Follow school directions about phones and electronic devices.
  • Do not use digital platforms to bully, threaten or impersonate others.
  • Respect personal information, images and account security.
  • Use school-approved technology for its intended educational purpose.
🤝

Parents, guardians and visitors

Positive cooperation between families and the school helps students succeed.

  • Use official school contact channels for enquiries and concerns.
  • Follow visitor sign-in and access procedures communicated by the school.
  • Discuss concerns respectfully with the appropriate teacher or administrator.
  • Confirm meeting arrangements before visiting when possible.
⚖️

Concerns, complaints and discipline

Concerns should be raised through appropriate school channels and handled fairly.

  • Report concerns promptly to a teacher, year coordinator or school administrator.
  • Provide clear, factual information and allow the school to review the matter.
  • Follow the school’s authorised procedures for complaints and disciplinary matters.
  • Seek clarification from the administration about applicable procedures and review options.

Need the official policy or a clarification?

For current approved rules, formal procedures, or advice about a specific situation, contact Lae Secondary School administration. The summary on this page should not be treated as a complete statement of school regulations.
